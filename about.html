Our story

We think a good home shouldn’t take a year.

Kemas means “neat” in Malay — and that’s how we think renovation should feel. One team, one price, one date. Here’s how we got here.

2014

Two designers, one drafting table

We started in a shoplot in Petaling Jaya, redesigning rental units for landlords tired of empty listings. The brief was simple: spend less, rent faster.

2017

Bringing the build in-house

Missed deadlines from subcontractors were costing our clients money. So we hired our own carpenters and site crews — and made the 30-working-day promise.

2021

Themes, not blank pages

We turned our best-performing designs into pre-costed themes. Homeowners loved choosing from something real, and prices dropped by up to 70% versus full-custom ID.

Today

2,400 homes and counting

Seven regions, in-house crews in each, partner financing up to 84 months, and a leasing network that tenants 98% of our investor units.
