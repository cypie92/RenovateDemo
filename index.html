FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Ask a real designer — not a bot.

Nadia Azman Senior designer · replies within the hour
Ask Nadia
How can you finish in 30 working days?

Our themes are pre-designed and pre-costed, so there’s no months-long design phase. Materials are stocked in our warehouse, and the same in-house crews work every site. The 30 days counts from site handover, after your design is signed off.

Can I customise a theme?

Yes. Every theme is a starting point. Swap colours, finishes, furniture or layouts with your designer — your 3D render and quote update before you commit.

Is the estimate the final price?

The online estimate is a range based on similar recent projects. After a site visit, you get one itemised, fixed quote. If we missed something, that’s on us — not you.

Do you work outside the Klang Valley?

We cover Klang Valley, Penang, Johor Bahru, Ipoh, Melaka, Kota Kinabalu and Kuching, with in-house crews in each region.

Can you help me rent the unit out?

Yes. For investor makeovers, our leasing partners photograph, list and screen tenants for you. Ongoing property management is available as an add-on.

What payment options do I have?

Pay in stages (30% / 50% / 20%), use 0% instalment plans on selected credit cards, or finance up to 84 months through our partner financiers.
