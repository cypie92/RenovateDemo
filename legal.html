Privacy notice (PDPA 2010)

  • What we collect: the name, mobile number, email and property details you give us in the estimator or contact form.
  • Why: only to reply to your enquiry, prepare your quote and run your project. We never sell your data.
  • Who sees it: your designer and project team, and — only if you ask for financing — the financier you choose.
  • How long: enquiries without a project are deleted after 12 months; project records are kept for 7 years as required by law.
  • Your rights: ask to see, correct or delete your data any time at privacy@kemas.studio.

Website terms

  • Delivery dates run from site handover and are stated in your quote; the late-handover payment applies as written there.
  • Financing is provided by third-party financiers and subject to their approval. Rates shown are illustrations.
  • Images show finished themes and may include styling items not in the standard package.
